The brief

  • ✓Clean and analyze a sales data file (retail sales data for the US).
  • ✓Produce clear Insights and recommendations in business language.
  • ✓Deliver a professional Excel dashboard with Pivot Tables.
  • ✓Client prefers Power Query for data transformation.

What I delivered

  • ✓Clean workbook: 9,644 transactions, zero-return rows removed, unique RowID added.
  • ✓Excel with 5 real PivotTables + charts (region, retailer, product, method, monthly trend).
  • ✓Power Query (M) script to re-run the cleaning automatically on new data.
  • ✓Insights report: $899.9M sales, 36.9% margin, West leads, Online highest margin.
  • ✓Interactive Arabic RTL dashboard — 6 charts, live cross-filters, works on mobile.

Data findings

2021 sales ($718M) were about 4× 2020 ($182M), while margin also rose to 37.4% — growth that was both bigger and more profitable. The West region leads at $270M, and West Gear + Foot Locker together account for 51% of revenue.

Men's Street Footwear is the top product ($209M), and Online is the highest-margin channel (39%). The Midwest is the weakest region — the clearest growth opportunity.

The dashboard lets you verify every one of these findings yourself: pick any region, store, product or channel and every chart updates instantly.
