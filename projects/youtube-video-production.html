Description

Kodak invented the first digital camera in 1975. Then they buried it to protect film profits. In 1975, 24-year-old engineer Steve Sasson built the world's first digital camera at Kodak. It weighed 3.6 kg, took 23 seconds per photo, and captured 0.01 megapixels. By 2012, Kodak filed for bankruptcy with $7 billion in debt.
