TerraGrow — Organic Fertilizer Ad Banner

A 1200×628 Facebook ad that tells the growth story of an organic fertilizer brand — from fertile soil to vibrant new growth.

The brief

  • ✓Promote an organic fertilizer brand (TerraGrow).
  • ✓Exact palette: #A7C957 · #6A994E · #386641 · #1B4332.
  • ✓Poppins Bold & Regular typography.
  • ✓Copy: "Grow Naturally." · "100% organic plant boosters." · "Order Now."
  • ✓Soil-texture background with a vibrant green CTA zone.

What I delivered

  • ✓1200×628 PNG, pixel-accurate and ready to upload.
  • ✓Natural dark-soil texture built in brand tones.
  • ✓Fresh green sprout as the visual anchor.
  • ✓Vibrant green CTA zone with "Order Now." (5.87:1 contrast).
  • ✓High-contrast text, WCAG-checked, ad-friendly text ratio.
  • ✓Delivery summary message for handover.

Design rationale

In organic-farming marketing, the soil is the hero — it signals origin, purity and life. While most competitors show a generic leaf, this design leads with a fertile soil texture that makes TerraGrow feel like the real, rooted choice.

The green palette already tells the growth story: from deep fertile soil (#1B4332) up to vibrant new growth (#A7C957). The banner guides the eye exactly that path — soil → sprout → headline → CTA — with a bold green CTA zone that pins the eye on "Order Now."

Short, high-contrast text keeps the ad clean and performs better on Meta (images with under 20% text rank better).
